My Story

I'm Trinh Huu Tho, a final-year Computer Science student at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Education (HCMUTE) 🇻🇳. I'm passionate about building intelligent systems at the intersection of research and real-world engineering.

My journey into AI began with a deep fascination for Natural Language Processing. I've worked on machine translation, Vietnamese sentiment analysis, sign language translation, and LLM-powered applications. I enjoy tackling challenging problems that bridge cutting-edge research with practical software solutions.

Beyond coursework, I actively participate in hackathons (🏆 1st Prize – Hackathon HCMUTE 2025), contribute to open-source projects, and share knowledge through blog posts. When I'm not coding, you'll find me enjoying a cup of matcha.
