My Story

I'm Trinh Huu Tho, an AI Engineer based in Vietnam 🇻🇳. I graduated with a degree in Computer Science and quickly found my passion at the intersection of research and engineering — building AI systems that actually work in production.

My journey into AI started with a fascination for Natural Language Processing. Over the years, I've expanded into Computer Vision, Reinforcement Learning, and most recently, Large Language Models and AI Agents. I've had the privilege of working on projects ranging from customer service automation to medical image analysis.

I'm a strong believer in open-source development and regularly contribute to the community through code, blog posts, and speaking at local meetups. When I'm not in front of a screen, you'll find me hiking, reading sci-fi, or experimenting with new recipes.
