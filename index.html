About Me

A passionate AI Engineer turning complex research into real-world solutions

Hello, I'm Trinh Huu Tho 👋

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at HCMUTE (Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Education), specializing in Artificial Intelligence. I'm passionate about NLP, Generative AI, and building intelligent systems that solve real problems.

From fine-tuning language models and building RAG pipelines to deploying bilingual machine translation systems, I bring both research curiosity and practical engineering to every project.

When I'm not training models, I write about AI on my blog and participate in hackathons. I believe in making AI accessible, understandable, and genuinely useful.
